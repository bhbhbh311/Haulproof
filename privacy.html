Privacy policy

Callahan Transportation, Inc. · HaulProof (haulproofepod.com) · Updated November 2026

What we collect

To move freight we keep business contact details for the shippers, receivers, carriers and drivers we work with: names, company names, emails, phone numbers, and load details such as addresses, times, truck and trailer numbers, and signed paperwork (BOLs and PODs).

In the HaulProof driver app we also keep the driver's photos of signed paperwork, signatures captured on the phone, and the phone's location at the moment the driver completes a pickup or delivery step. We don't track location at any other time.

How we use it

Only to arrange, track, document, bill and pay for loads, and to keep the records the law asks us to keep. Driver and dispatcher mobile numbers are used to text about the loads they're hauling for us (see HaulProof Driver Text Alerts).

Text messages

We text only numbers given to us for a load, with the consent described on our text alerts page. Reply STOP to any text to stop them, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers, text-message opt-in or consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are never shared with anyone except the phone companies and the texting provider (Twilio) that deliver our messages.

Who we share it with

We share load details only with the people working on that load (the shipper, receiver, carrier and driver) and with the companies that run our systems for us: email, texting, hosting and accounting.

How long we keep it, and keeping it safe

We keep load records as long as tax and transportation rules require. Our systems need a login or a personal link that is hard to guess, drivers protect their app with their own PIN, and connections are encrypted.

Your choices
