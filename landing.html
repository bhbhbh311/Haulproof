Everything in one place

Proof of delivery, without the paperwork chase

Stop hunting for scanned BOLs and chasing drivers for signatures. HaulProof captures it once, cleanly, and routes it everywhere it needs to go.

Sign on any phone

Drivers open a link — no app store, no login friction. The signature lands exactly where you placed it on the real document, and a Daylight mode keeps it readable in full sun.

Multi-stop loads

One PO, many stops. Each stop keeps its own document and signature, grouped under a single load so nothing gets lost.

Instant notifications

The second a stop is signed, the right people get it — the stop's sales rep, load subscribers, and an always-notify master list.

Dispatch to the driver

Assign a load to one of your drivers and it appears on their phone, ready to sign. One driver per load, no mix-ups.

Receiver directory

Keep your delivery locations in one place, import them in bulk, and share a global list every customer can pull from.

Full audit trail

Every load has a timeline — created, prepared, assigned, signed, emailed — with the signing location pinned on a map.

AI places the signatures

AI reads each new document and puts the signature, date and time boxes where they belong. Save the layout once and every load after it is set up instantly, for free.

A dashboard that says what's next

Open the portal to see what's on the road, what's ready, and what needs you: setups to finish, loads without a driver, emails that bounced. Each one is a click from fixed.

Clean paperwork, every time

Remove the terms, mill certs and invoices the receiver never signs, split a scan into stops, crop a photo, or send a blurry page back to the driver to re-shoot.

How it works

From dock to inbox in three steps

No hardware, no scanners, no paper shuffle. Just a link and a signature.

1

Upload & prepare

Drop in the BOL or POD, remove the pages you don't need, and place the signature boxes — or let AI and your saved layouts do it for you.

2

Assign & dispatch

Assign the load to a driver. It shows up on their phone under the PO#, with each stop ready to sign.

3

Sign & deliver

The driver checks in, the receiver signs on the phone, and the signed document emails itself to everyone who needs it — instantly.
