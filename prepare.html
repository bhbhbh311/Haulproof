Sign + date
  1. Pick who signs. Tap a Signer at the top — Consignee, Driver, Shipper/Pickup, or ＋ Add signer for anyone else. The selected one shows a ✓.
  2. Pick what they do. Tap a Field — Sign + date, Signature, Initials, Date, Time, or Text box. The selected one shows a ✓.
  3. Place it. Tap the document where that field should go. A colored box (in the signer's color) drops there.
  4. Adjust it. Drag the box to move it, pull the corner dot to resize, or tap the red × to remove it.
  5. Repeat for each signer and field, then tap Save setup (top right). It's now ready for the driver to sign.
Tip: drag a box to move it, pull its corner dot to resize, or select it and press Delete.
