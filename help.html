New-company setup Admins

Getting a new carrier or account up and running. Do these once, in order.

  1. Sign in to the portal. Go to haulproofepod.com/app. The first time, use the sign-in link that was emailed to you (or the temporary password), then choose your own password.
  2. Add your team. In the left menu, open Team logins and add your dispatchers and sales reps. Each person gets their own sign-in link automatically. They set their own password the first time they open it.
  3. Add your drivers. Open Drivers and add each driver (name, plus phone or email if you like). Each driver gets a personal link; click Copy link next to their name and text it to them. They open it on their phone, with no app to install.
  4. Add your customers. Open Customers, add the companies you haul for, and for each one set the signed-document recipients (who gets the finished BOL) and the sales reps who own that customer. (See the Customers guide below.)
  5. Check your settings. Settings lets you hide fields you don't use, turn the signature audit-trail page on or off, and choose whether drivers add pickup or delivery photos.
Tip: you can send new team members and drivers this Help page. The link is haulproofepod.com/help. To invite another company, use the Invite to HaulProof link at the bottom of the left menu.

Dispatchers & admins Portal

Everything you do from the dispatch portal: the Dashboard, building a load, preparing documents, and setting up signatures.

1. Sign in

Go to haulproofepod.com/app and enter your email and password. Forgot it? Use Forgot password? under the Sign in button. Never received a login? Ask your admin to resend your sign-in link.

2. The Dashboard

You land on the Dashboard. The line under the greeting sums up your day, and the tiles count your loads by status. Click any tile to open the Loads list showing just those loads.

Needs attention lists what's waiting on you, each with a button to fix it: emails that didn't go out (Resend), documents whose signature boxes aren't placed yet (Set up), and loads with no driver (Assign). Drivers today shows who's on the road, who's assigned, and who has finished.

3. Find and open a load

Open Documents / Loads in the left menu. Each load shows a status pill:

Needs setup a driver uploaded paperwork and it needs signature boxes   Ready to sign on the driver's phone   1 of 2 stops signed part done   Completed every stop signed   Signed · Email failed the signed copy didn't go out   Open no paperwork released yet

Use the filter chips above the list (Needs setup, On the road, Email failed…) or search by PO #. The newest 10 loads show first; search to find older ones. Archive hides a load from your list without deleting it.

4. Start a new load

Click ＋ New load on the Dashboard (or the New load box on Documents / Loads). Enter the customer PO #, upload the BOL now or add it later, and the load is created and searchable right away.

5. Edit the load

Open the load and click ✎ Edit load / stops. Everything is on one screen and saves as you go:

  1. PO # / Load # and the Customer it’s for.
  2. Stops & documents: each stop’s document type, Delivered to (the receiver) and Sales rep. Add or split stops here.
  3. Assign a driver. The load goes to their phone.
  4. Load updates: tick the team members who should get an email as each stop completes.

6. Prepare documents (remove pages, split into stops)

A customer's PDF often comes with extra pages (terms, mill certs, invoices). On the document row click 📄 Review to open Prepare documents. Every page shows as a card:

  1. Remove page takes pages the driver doesn't need out of the document. Removed a page by mistake? Click it in the red Removed bar to bring it back.
  2. Stop # on each page splits a multi-stop scan into one document per stop, with Stop 1 first.
  3. Crop cleans up a page, and Re-shoot sends a bad page back to the driver to photograph again.

7. Set up the signatures

On the document row click ✍ Setup signatures. On the left, pick who signs (Consignee, Driver, Shipper, or add your own) and what goes on the page, then click on the document where it goes. Drag a box to move it, pull its corner to resize, or hover and click × to remove it.

  • Signatures: Sign + date, Signature, Initials.
  • Fills in automatically: Date, Pickup date, Pickup in / out and Delivery in / out times. HaulProof stamps these when the driver signs or checks in.
  • Text box: type a value (like a seal or trailer #) right on the desktop, and it prints on the document.

Click Save setup when it looks right. The document goes straight to the assigned driver’s phone to sign.

Do it once per customer: saved layouts. When you save, HaulProof asks whether to Remember it for that customer. Next time, open Layouts ▾ and choose Apply saved layout, or let HaulProof apply it for you. It’s instant and free. (A layout needs a customer on the load.)
Auto-place: the violet Auto-place button (✨ AI place when AI is on) reads the document and places the boxes for you. With AI turned on for your account, it uses Claude to find every signature line. Check the boxes, then Save, and remember the layout so AI isn't needed next time.

8. Signed copies and failed emails

When a stop is signed, HaulProof emails the signed copy to the customer’s recipients. If an email can’t be delivered, the document shows Email failed with the reason, HaulProof retries automatically for several hours, and it appears under Needs attention on the Dashboard. Click ↻ Resend to try again now, or ✉ Send on any document to email it again.

Drivers On your phone

No app to install. Your dispatcher sends you a link. Open it on your phone and you’re in.

  1. Open the link your dispatcher sent you in your phone’s browser, and set your PIN the first time. If it came by text, tap the whole link so none of it gets cut off.
  2. Turn on location when asked. It’s only read when you check in and sign. That’s your proof the load reached the right place.
  3. Tap a load under Your loads to open it. Loads dispatch has assigned you show up here on their own.
  4. At pickup, tap Check in at pickup when you arrive and Pickup complete when you’re loaded.
  5. At each delivery stop, tap Check in at Stop 1, then Open, sign & complete Stop 1. Have the receiver sign where the boxes are, tap Finish, and add any extra email addresses for the signed copy. Signing checks you out of the stop.
Got paperwork dispatch doesn’t have? Tap Upload for dispatch to set up, take clear photos of every page, and dispatch will place the signature boxes and send it back to you to sign.
Blurry photos? Move closer until the page fills the outline and hold still for a second. That’s the biggest fix for hard-to-read documents. Everything works offline and uploads once you have signal.

Customers & receivers Signed docs

If you’re a customer or a delivery location, you usually don’t log in day to day. Signed documents come to you by email automatically.

How signed documents reach you

The moment a stop is signed, HaulProof emails the completed document to the recipients set up for that customer. To control who gets them, the carrier opens Customers, edits the customer, and under Signed-document recipients ticks Gets docs for each person. Receivers (delivery locations) can also have their own BOL contacts.

Sales reps: reps assigned to a customer see that customer’s loads in their list by default and get an email when its stops complete, so a rep only deals with their own accounts.
Didn’t get a document? Ask the carrier to check the load. If the email bounced, it shows Email failed with the reason, and they can resend it with one click.

AI assistant Optional

Connect HaulProof to Claude (or any AI app that supports MCP connectors) and just tell it what you need.

  1. On the Dashboard, find Let AI do the paperwork and click Copy connector address.
  2. In your AI app, add a custom connector, paste the address, and sign in with your HaulProof login.
  3. Attach your BOLs and say something like “Here are 3 Brown Strauss BOLs for PO 4821, one stop each, send to Mike.” It creates the load, keeps only the pages to sign, places the signature boxes, and sends it to the driver.
Cost: every account starts with a free AI credit to try it. Your admin can see what’s left, and ask for more, under Settings → AI features. Once a customer’s layout is saved, HaulProof reuses it for free and doesn’t need AI again. Manage or disconnect AI apps under Settings → AI connections.

Troubleshooting Quick fixes

The handful of things that trip people up most, and how to clear them fast.

A link says “page not found”

Usually the link is old or got cut off in a text message. Ask for a fresh link and copy-paste the whole thing into your browser.

“This link is not valid / expired”

Sign-in links expire after 7 days. Ask your admin to resend it (or reset your password) and use the new one.

A driver uploaded a document but can’t sign it yet

That’s expected. It waits as Needs setup until a dispatcher places the signature boxes and clicks Save setup. Then it shows up on the driver’s phone to sign.

The driver can’t change the PO # or customer

Once dispatch has prepared a document, its details are locked on the driver’s phone so nothing gets changed by mistake. The driver should call dispatch, and dispatch edits it from the load.

A signed copy shows “Email failed”

The email service refused it. The reason shows on the document (for example a mistyped address or a full mailbox). Fix the recipient on the customer if needed, then click ↻ Resend. HaulProof also retries on its own for several hours.

The document has pages the driver doesn’t need

Click 📄 Review on the document and use Remove page. Removed one by mistake? Click it in the Removed bar to restore it.

Can’t log in at all

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page. If you never got a login, your admin can create one or resend your link from Team logins. Admins can also reset anyone’s password there.

Scanned documents are blurry

Get closer so the page fills the frame, hold the phone steady, and make sure the whole page is inside the outline before you take the photo.

A PO # shows a duplicate warning

If a document was already waiting on that PO, HaulProof flags a possible duplicate. Dispatch can Keep it (if it really is a second document) or Discard it.

I deleted a load or document by mistake

Admins can open Recovery bin at the top right of the Loads list and click Restore. Deleted items are kept for a year.
