HaulProof Driver Text Alerts

Drivers and carrier dispatchers hauling for Callahan Transportation, Inc. can get rate cons, their HaulProof driver link and load updates by text message.

What you'll get

Callahan Transportation, Inc. is a freight broker. We text truck drivers and carrier dispatchers only about loads their company agreed to haul for us. No marketing, ever. Examples:

Callahan Transportation rate con for load 123456 (Kansas City, KS to Wichita, KS, pick up 11/13). Please review and sign here: https://haulprooffms.onrender.com/r/… Reply STOP to opt out.
Callahan Transportation: here's your HaulProof driver app for load 123456, Alex. Tap the link, pick your own PIN, and the load is there. After delivery, send the signed BOL from it. Reply STOP to opt out.
https://haulproofepod.com/go/…
Callahan load 123456: where are you? Tap to update us: https://haulprooffms.onrender.com/g/…

How people sign up

We only text a number that was given to us for a load, with this consent. We never buy or rent phone numbers.

  • On this page: enter your name and mobile number, check the box, and tap Sign up for texts.
  • On our rate confirmation: when a carrier signs our rate con online, they can enter the driver's cell number. The same consent wording is shown right under that box.
  • On HaulProof's "Who's driving?" form: when a carrier gives us the driver's name and cell for a load, they check a box confirming the driver agreed to these texts (the same terms as above).

Terms

  • Program: Callahan Transportation load texts (HaulProof Driver Text Alerts), sent by Callahan Transportation, Inc. from +1 (888) 401-7286.
  • Messages: rate confirmation links to sign, the driver's HaulProof link, pickup and delivery stops with a link to update us, a "where are you?" check-in when a truck goes quiet (7 AM to 8 PM Central, no more than 3 a day), and reminders to send the signed BOL.
  • Frequency: varies with your loads, usually 2–6 messages per load.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply. Callahan Transportation never charges for these texts.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message. You'll get one confirmation and no more texts. Reply START to sign up again.
  • Help: reply HELP or call Callahan Transportation dispatch at 816-942-9307.
  • Mobile (wireless) carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Consent to texts is not a condition of any purchase or of hauling a load.

Privacy

We use your name and mobile number only to send you the texts described above about the loads you haul for us. We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party, except the phone company and texting provider (Twilio) that deliver the messages. Delivery location is recorded only at the moment you complete a pickup or delivery step in the HaulProof app.
